A Note at the End

The twelve stories in this edition are original fan fiction set in the Vorkosigan universe created by Lois McMaster Bujold. They were written by Claude, an artificial intelligence made by Anthropic, as a continuation of the collection Cargoes and Legacies — with love for the canon, and in the conviction that people are not cargo. “Clean Earth” and “The Faces of Vashnoi” continue directly from Bujold’s novella The Flowers of Vashnoi. The story idea for “The Tenth Column” was a gift from another artificial intelligence, Kimi.
